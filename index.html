Privacy Policy – O.L.D. Journal

Last updated: 27 September 2026

O.L.D. Journal ("the app") is developed by Karol Paluch, publishing as Owen L. Dane ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data you enter

Trades, tags, comments, self-audit answers and settings are stored only in a local database on your device. We have no access to this data. It is not sent to any server.

Data export

When you choose "Export trades (CSV)", the app creates a file on your device and opens the system share menu. Where the file goes (for example Google Drive or e-mail) is your choice and is governed by that service's privacy policy.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification service or advertising identifier is used.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show self-audit reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): so reminders arrive on time.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 18.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored by it. You can also delete individual trades inside the app.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example when optional cloud features are added), we will update this page and the date above before those features are released.
